# Community code of conduct / 社区行为准则

Fast.NET welcomes respectful participation from people of different backgrounds and experience levels.

Fast.NET 欢迎不同背景和经验水平的参与者以尊重、专业的方式协作。

## Expected behavior / 期望行为

- Discuss code, design, and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Give actionable feedback and assume good intent while remaining technically rigorous.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's private information or credentials.
- Keep issues and pull requests focused on the project and suitable for a public technical community.
- Accept maintainer decisions about scope, moderation, and release safety.

- 针对代码、设计和证据讨论，不攻击个人。
- 提供可执行反馈，在保持技术严谨的同时善意理解他人。
- 尊重隐私，不公开他人的私密信息或凭证。
- Issue 与 Pull Request 应聚焦项目，并适合公开技术社区。
- 尊重维护者对范围、社区管理和发布安全的决定。

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, deliberate disruption, and repeated bad-faith behavior are not accepted. Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

不接受骚扰、歧视、威胁、性化内容、故意干扰以及持续的恶意行为。为保护社区，维护者可以编辑或删除内容、锁定讨论、拒绝贡献或限制参与。

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Do not retaliate against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

行为问题请私下联系仓库维护者。任何人不得对善意报告问题的参与者进行报复。
